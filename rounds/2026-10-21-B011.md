# B011: readable compact challenge details

Baseline and rollback: `a208b66dd15db20f69ff16fc0702a65a184d3002` (3.3.18).

Self-play on 2026-10-21 from 06:49:47 to 06:50:29 UTC used eighteen actual
touch swaps across six viewports.
At 321x320 the full challenge line was almost unreadably small; at 460x459 it
sat against the top of the Play row. The existing full-heading side plaque
correctly declined these layouts because its words could not fit.

B011 keeps every successful existing side layout. When that layout declines,
the narrow-fit presentation can use a short Moves, Timed, Daily or Puzzle
heading with the complete caption at 11px. Whole words and full numbers wrap
onto separate lines. Nothing shrinks, truncates or creates a new input target.
The original drawing remains if there is not enough room for the complete
caption, its plaque and shadow. The board, camera, controls and authoritative
run stay unchanged.

The short headings intentionally omit the decorative puzzle name and daily
date. Puzzle goals and remaining counts stay in the title. The daily board's
date remains in its opening notice and Play context. This is a compact status
tradeoff, not a claim that every original heading is retained.

The actual Windows font measures Resonance at 79.308px for an 11px cap height.
That word cannot fit the safe side gutter at 321x320 or 460x459. Those timed
captions keep their complete original drawing; move budgets and ordinary
ghost values gain the readable plaque. Oversized ghost values also retain
the original fallback.

Independent source review checked 29,269 eligible integer viewports and found
at least 3.55px between the new plaque shadow and the tray under maximum
camera punch and independent shake/roll. Browser gates check actual glyph
widths, real swaps, all challenge modes, private co-op, comfort settings and
excluded surfaces.

All 642 candidate checks pass: 47 CPU, 201 focused WebGPU, 201 focused WebGL,
22 normal gameplay, 112 mobile/UI, 48 progression/save/mode/photo checks and
11 focused private co-op checks. Another 33 baseline checks provide nine
matched views with identical game state, board/camera geometry, title/score
placement and all normal input targets. Existing successful status drawings
also match exactly. The normalized runtime matches the baseline byte for byte
after substituting only the status presentation source and version.

The focused co-op harness initially assumed the wider compact results card
would also apply at 321px and 460px. The baseline deliberately uses its original
result renderer below 480px. Independent review confirmed the corrected gate:
status plaques clear on completion and both original continuation actions
remain present. The first failure receipt is retained; no game fix was needed.
