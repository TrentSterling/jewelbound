# B021: achievement notices clear of About on short phones

Baseline and rollback: `d850b37722c636ea2489398073d40e9dc2bf69e8` (3.3.28).

Fresh self-play on 2026-10-21 at 16:55:25 to 16:56:05 UTC exercised twelve
actual touch swaps, four puzzle solves, four Hints and twenty-four menu actions
across four viewports. At 320x480 the puzzle-completion achievement toast was
partly covered by About. Measurements ruled out adding a top banner because
the result card begins too close to the top of the screen.

B021 relocates only a complete, fitting notice into the unused gap between
the result reason and score when the original toast overlaps About on short
portrait screens. Actual cached atlas ink metrics, including display-font
score shrink and existing shadows, determine the gap. Notice rows use 11px
body text without truncation. Bounds must clear the viewport, About and controls.
The text is drawn after the unchanged result card with the original opacity.
The original toast path owns its lifetime and accessible announcement. Pending
state resets each frame and is cleared before result drawing, even on errors.

The change is restricted to portrait widths 280 through 479 and heights 460
through 639. Short squares use a different result card and retain their original
path. Menus, photo/AA/practice/auto modes, noncolliding and unfittable messages
also retain their original behavior. No results, hit targets, rules, balances,
saves, shared authority, audio, assets or dependencies change. Removing the
new presentation source and restoring the version exactly reconstructs the
normalized B020 runtime.
