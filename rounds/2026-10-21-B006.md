# B006: room for challenge results

Baseline and rollback: `379cc6b73c489cec734c063bbcb84cbc4f0efa28` (3.3.13).

Self-play completed twelve real mouse/touch swaps across desktop, phone and
landscape, including a final move that opened each challenge result. The short
landscape result was a tall narrow card crowding the score and covering the
control rail. Baseline raw screenshots and timestamps are retained locally.

The result renderer now uses two columns where a landscape card has enough room:
score and statistics on the left, the original continuation actions on the right.
It keeps the complete surface and its shadow above the actual HUD hit targets,
and reserves the narrow screen's top corner for About. Buttons remain 42px tall.
Ordinary portrait and desktop, open panels, unfinished runs and layouts with
insufficient space delegate to the original renderer. No input handler changes.

Independent review caught hidden HUD targets under the first wider candidate.
The revised geometry clears them. The reviewer independently swept 1,644 runtime
viewports: 1,206 compact layouts cleared every target and 438 delegated. The
reported 568x320, 480x320 and 844x240 cases passed. At 844x180, the old renderer is
retained. Fresh real-touch checks also target the result's blank background.

The retained original result at 844x180 still has an off-screen continuation
action. Its rendering is compared with baseline; the audit explicitly excludes
that action from passing touch claims. This round does not expand into that
extreme-height layout.

Removing only the new result module and version increment reproduces the complete
baseline runtime exactly. Rules, save keys, networking, controls, photo behavior
and approved audio bytes remain unchanged. No assets or dependencies were added.
