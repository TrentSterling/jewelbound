# B017: readable challenge captions on compact phones

Baseline and rollback: `b20b9a8ef57ef0630e3cf4188ef997b58e824bdb` (3.3.24).

Fresh self-play on 2026-10-21 at 12:47:55 to 12:48:23 UTC used eight real
touch swaps, four Hint activations and twenty menu actions across four
viewports. At 320x480, the challenge caption crossed the top of Hint and
Play. The original challenge row also reduced its font to fit long values.

B017 reserves a readable challenge row only when an upright portrait tray
already crowds its original control rail. Complete words wrap into at most
two lines at 11px. Shared camera framing gives the tray, picking rays and
stable HUD the same layout, including clearance for camera punch and shake.
The original normal and AA control rectangles are preserved. All caption
values remain complete, with the original fallback for unsupported content.

The fitting branch is limited to portrait widths below 600px and heights
of at least 460px. Already readable layouts and existing landscape framing
remain exact. No rules, saves, network authority, voice, assets or controls
change. Removing the new presentation source, AA placement hook and version
increment reproduces the B016 runtime exactly.

first candidate. Responsive spacing replaced its fixed gap. The failed
candidate and test receipt are retained separately from final evidence.
Validation covers fixed-size complete captions, original input rectangles,
bottom-row touch selection and swaps, daily/timed/puzzle modes, ghost values,
high contrast, reduced motion, rotation, AA controls, photo mode, successful
