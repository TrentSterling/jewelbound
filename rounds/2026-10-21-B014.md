# B014: reachable menu return on short screens

Baseline and rollback: `5e6639c3c806e0306c880978fa17974df7780629` (3.3.21).

Fresh self-play on 2026-10-21 from 09:56:10 to 09:56:32 UTC made six actual
touch swaps across portrait, desktop and short landscape screens. The short
Play menu showed Back beneath the DOM About launcher. Further actual-touch
probes reproduced the obstruction at 568x320, 390x320 and 320x320: tapping
the Back target opened About and left the game menu open.

B014 measures the real About launcher and reflows only colliding menu
headers. Back and Audio retain their original 76x37 target, complete label
and action, with six pixels of clearance from About. The heading keeps its
original size where it fits, otherwise wrapping the complete text above the
existing tabs. Body geometry, tab targets, scroll limits and state stay
unchanged. Photo mode, open About and noncolliding headers delegate to the
original renderer. Temporary drawing hooks restore even after an exception.

The before/after composite uses real browser captures of the same seed 1,
one touch swap, score 150, Play menu at 568x320 and announcer menu at 320x320.
It shows the old obstruction alongside the reachable controls. The broader
audit records nine valid menus across eight viewports on both renderers.
Independent review caught an invalid `graphics` test tab; those initial
scenarios are excluded from counts and replaced with the actual `display`
tab in supplemental receipts. Each full audit records 72 scenarios and
24 saved PNGs, not 72 screenshots.

The first extra-interaction audit reused About's closed-summary position
after its details expanded. That test correctly failed to close About;
the corrected harness remeasures the open summary before the second tap.
The failed receipt is retained. No runtime change was needed.

Rules, saves, co-op, original assets and all prior runtime code remain
byte-identical after normalizing the new presentation snippet and version.
Physical phones, Safari and hardware controllers remain manual. Extremely
small screens without space for the original return target retain prior
rendering; supported 320px layouts keep complete readable headings.
